1. Introduction 
1.1 Purpose 
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the functional and non-functional 
requirements for a GST Sales Billing Software designed to create, save, manage, search, 
print, and export sales invoices. 
The system will provide a simple billing dashboard and an invoice creation workflow while 
keeping the system lightweight by not maintaining a separate product master database. 
Products/items will be entered directly while creating an invoice. Product information will be 
stored as part of the relevant invoice for historical, reporting, and audit purposes only. 
 
2. Project Objective 
The primary objective of the system is to provide a simple, responsive, and mobile-friendly 
billing solution that allows authorized users to: 
1. Create GST sales invoices. 
2. Enter customer information when required. 

3. Add products/items directly into an invoice without maintaining a separate product 
database. 
4. Automatically calculate quantity, rate, discount, taxable value, GST, round-off, and grand 
total. 
5. Select applicable GST rates such as 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%, etc. 
6. Allow a custom GST rate where required. 
7. Validate business and customer GSTIN using basic GSTIN format validation. 
8. Save invoices for future reference. 
9. Search and manage previously created invoices. 
10. Edit or cancel invoices according to user permissions. 
11. Automatically reflect invoice edits in related sales and GST reports. 
12. Maintain invoice edit activity in the audit/activity log. 
13. Export complete invoices within a selected date range. 
14. Export invoice audit reports within a selected date range. 
15. Provide separate access panels for Admin, Owner, and Staff users.

4. Product Database Restriction 
The system shall not maintain a separate Product Master/Product Database. 
Users shall not be required to: 
● Create products in advance 
● Save products for future selection 
● Maintain inventory 
● Maintain stock quantities 
● Update stock 
● Search products from a permanent product catalog 
Products/items shall instead be entered directly during invoice creation. 
Once an invoice is saved, its product information shall remain associated with that invoice for 
historical records, reports, and audits. It shall not automatically become a reusable product 
record.

10. Business GSTIN Validation 
The system shall perform basic format validation for the Business GSTIN. 
The validation shall verify that the entered GSTIN: 
● Contains 15 characters. 
● Follows the expected alphanumeric GSTIN structure. 
● Does not contain invalid characters. 
● Is not obviously malformed. 
This shall be considered format validation only. 
The system shall not treat successful format validation as proof that the GSTIN is officially active 
or registered unless a separate government/API verification service is implemented.

registration verification without external GST integration.

The invoice item GST field shall provide commonly applicable GST rate options. 
Standard GST Rate Options

18. Custom GST Rate 
In addition to predefined GST rates, the system shall provide a Custom GST Rate option. 
When Custom GST is selected, the authorized user may manually enter the required GST 
percentage. 
Example 

GST Rate → Custom → 3% 
The system shall then use the entered custom percentage for the corresponding item's GST 
calculation. 
The system should validate that the entered custom GST rate is a valid non-negative 
percentage within the application's configured limits. 
 
19. GST Calculation 
The system shall automatically calculate applicable GST values. 
Required Components 
● Taxable Amount 
● CGST 
● SGST 
● IGST 
● Total Tax 
● Grand Total 
Intra-State Transaction 
For applicable intra-state transactions: 
GST = CGST + SGST 
The GST amount shall be appropriately divided between CGST and SGST. 
Inter-State Transaction 
For applicable inter-state transactions: 
GST = IGST 
The system shall automatically calculate the relevant tax based on the selected GST rate. 
 
20. Automatic Calculation 

The system shall automatically calculate: 
Quantity × Rate → Gross Amount 
Gross Amount − Discount → Taxable Value 
Taxable Value × Applicable GST Rate → GST Amount 
The system shall then calculate: 
Taxable Value + GST + Round-Off = Grand Total 
The exact calculation sequence shall be consistently applied across invoice creation, editing, 
reporting, and exports. 
 
21. Discount Calculation 
The system shall support discount entry at the invoice-item level. 
The system shall calculate: 
Gross Amount → Discount → Taxable Value → GST → Final Amount

The invoice shall display: 
● Subtotal

● Taxable Amount 
● Tax

27. Invoice Editing 
Authorized users shall be able to edit saved invoices according to their permissions. 
When an invoice is edited: 
1. The invoice data shall be updated. 
2. GST and financial calculations shall be recalculated. 
3. Related sales reports shall reflect the updated invoice. 
4. Related GST reports shall reflect the updated GST values. 
5. Dashboard figures based on the invoice shall be updated accordingly. 
6. The invoice edit shall be recorded in the activity/audit log. 
The reports must always reflect the latest valid state of the invoice. 
 
28. Invoice Edit Audit Log 
Every invoice edit shall maintain an activity log entry. 

The activity log should contain, where applicable: 
● Invoice Number 
● User Name/User ID 
● Action: Invoice Edited 
● Date 
● Time 
● Previous values or changed fields 
● Updated values, where technically supported 
● Final invoice amount 
● Final GST amount 
This shall provide traceability for invoice modifications. 
 
29. Report Synchronization After Editing

● Daily Sales Report

● Customer-wise Sales Report 
● GST Summary 
● CGST Summary 
● SGST Summary 
● IGST Summary

● Dashboard sales figures 
● Dashboard GST figures

52. Business Rules 
1. Customer information shall be optional. 
2. Products shall be entered manually during invoice creation. 
3. Products shall not be stored in a separate reusable product database. 
4. Product details shall remain associated with their respective invoices. 
5. Standard GST rates such as 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28% shall be available. 
6. The system shall support custom GST rates. 
7. GST shall be automatically calculated according to the selected rate. 
8. Business GSTIN shall undergo basic format validation. 
9. Customer GSTIN shall undergo basic format validation where entered. 
10. GSTIN format validation shall not imply government registration verification. 
11. Invoice numbers shall follow the configured number series. 
12. Invoice edits shall recalculate relevant financial and GST values. 
13. Invoice edits shall automatically update related sales and GST reports. 
14. Invoice edits shall be recorded in the audit/activity log. 
15. Cancelled invoices shall remain identifiable in invoice history. 
16. Invoice exports shall be based on selected date ranges. 
17. Complete invoice export shall contain all selected invoices in one PDF. 
18. Every invoice in the complete invoice export shall occupy a separate page. 
19. Invoice audit export shall be a tabular PDF. 
20. Role permissions shall control sensitive invoice and system operations. 

21. The system shall not include inventory/stock management unless separately specified. 
 
53. Acceptance Criteria 
The system shall be considered functionally complete when the following scenario works 
successfully: 
A Staff or Owner user logs in → opens Create Invoice → optionally enters customer details → 
manually adds one or more products/items → enters quantity, rate, discount, HSN/SAC, and 
GST rate → selects a standard GST rate or enters a custom GST rate → system automatically 
calculates taxable amount, CGST/SGST/IGST, total GST, round-off, and grand total → user 
saves the invoice → invoice appears in history → user can open, print, or download the invoice. 
The system shall also validate the format of any entered business or customer GSTIN. 
When the invoice is subsequently edited, the system shall recalculate the invoice, update the 
relevant sales/GST reports and dashboard values, and create an invoice-edit entry in the 
activity/audit log. 
The user shall then be able to select a date range and export all matching invoices into one 
PDF with each invoice on a separate page. The same date range shall also be exportable as an 
invoice audit PDF containing the required tabular information. 
No product shall be created as a permanent reusable product-master record.

56. Final Product Definition 
The final product shall be a: 
Mobile-friendly GST Billing and Invoice Management Web Application with Admin, 
Owner, and Staff panels, focused on invoice creation, invoice storage, GST calculation, 
invoice management, reporting, auditing, and date-range-based exports without 
maintaining a separate product database. 
The core system philosophy shall be: 
Create Bills → Save Bills → Manage Bills → Update Bills → Update Reports → Audit 
Changes → Export Bills & Audit Reports 
rather than: 
Create Products → Maintain Inventory → Sell Products 
This distinction is a core requirement of the project.
